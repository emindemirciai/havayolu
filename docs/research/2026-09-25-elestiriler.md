### [critical] The adsb.lol rate budget does not fit the P0/P1/P2/P3 plan. The limit I observed was about 3x lower than assumed, and 429 responses have no Retry-After header (01-parca-1 §6 'Hız kontrolü', 'Öncelikli iş kuyruğu', 'Kapsama birleştirme ve bütçe'; 00-ortak-baglam 'Veri kaynakları' (adsb.lol bullet))
PROBLEM: The prompt assumes 1 req/s and one 60 NM P0 circle per station at 2–5 s. IST and SAW are 33.7 NM apart, so the 5 stations collapse to 4 circles. At 3 s that is 1.33 req/s, at 2 s 2.0 req/s and at 5 s 0.8 req/s, so P0 alone takes 80–200% of the assumed budget. That is before P2 (Turkey needs at least 2 circles of 250 NM), P1 and P3. For P1, the OpenAPI (https://api.adsb.lol/api/openapi.json) documents only single-value /v2/hex/{icao_hex} and no multi-hex query. That means 1 request per watched aircraft outside coverage: 20 aircraft/min is 0.33 req/s. Total is about 1.9–2.3 req/s with P0 at 3 s, roughly 2x the assumed budget. The README (https://github.com/adsblol/api) says only 'Rate limits are dynamic based on the environment load'. My probe on 2026-09-25 14:06–14:08 UTC from one IP: 3 requests at 3 s spacing all returned 200. Requests at about 2 s spacing mostly returned 429. After a burst, even a request 6 s later got 429. The sustained rate I observed was therefore about 0.33 req/s (one sample, not a documented number). The 429 is an nginx HTML page (Content-Type text/html) with no Retry-After header, so the prompt's 'Retry-After'a uy' path never fires and a JSON parser will throw. Also verified: /v2 'now' is epoch milliseconds (1790345213001), 'seen_pos' is seconds (max observed 55.35), the point radius maximum is 250 NM, and gzip works (68,222 B raw → 16,802 B).

### [critical] A feed or ingest outage makes every aircraft on final fire a false 'probable_landing' push (02-parca-2 §4 'Olaylar' → probable_landing; 'Durumlar' (ALÇAKTA_KAYIP → MUHTEMEL_İNİŞ))
PROBLEM: The rule is: last sample below 1,000 ft AGL, ≤8 km from ARP, descending, and no data for ≥90 s. Nothing separates one aircraft dropping below coverage from a system-wide data gap. A 429 storm, a 403 policy change, a 5xx, a worker restart during a compose deploy, a Redis OOM, or CPU starvation from a build on the VPS all make every aircraft on short final at every station meet the condition at once. Every follower then gets a 'muhtemel iniş' push, including for aircraft that go around. Given the measured coverage (no ground targets at IST), this path will be the main landing path, so false pushes will be frequent and highly visible.

Kaynak dönünce uçak yerde görülürse normal teker koyma akışı çalışır. ### [critical] One 512 MB Redis runs noeviction with AOF and holds high-churn live data; the stream is unbounded, GEO members never expire, and maxmemory is unset (01-parca-1 §2 'Redis ayarları'; §6 'Canlı durum (Redis)'; 02-parca-2 §4 'Girdi: ac:updates')
PROBLEM: BullMQ needs 'maxmemory-policy noeviction' and recommends AOF (https://docs.bullmq.io/guide/going-to-production). The prompt puts that instance under all the live state too.
(a) `ac:updates` has no MAXLEN/MINID. At the design target (~250 updates/s × ~300 B) that is about 6.5 GB/day; at measured coverage (~40/s) about 1 GB/day (estimates). With noeviction, Redis then rejects every write: XADD fails so ingest stops, and BullMQ adds fail so notifications are lost. The prompt sets the policy but not a maxmemory value, so Redis may instead grow to the 512 MB cgroup limit and be SIGKILLed.
(b) GEO set members do not expire with the 120 s TTL on `ac:{hex}`. GEOSEARCH returns ghost aircraft and memory leaks slowly.
(c) AOF on data that lives 120 s: about 600 B of AOF per aircraft update is about 13 GB/day of appends at the target, which triggers frequent AOF rewrites. Redis docs: during an AOF rewrite a write-heavy Redis 'can use up to 2 times the memory normally used'. They also recommend vm.overcommit_memory=1 and swap (https://redis.io/docs/latest/operate/oss_and_stack/management/admin/). Fork copy-on-write spikes inside a 512 MB limit lead to OOM kills.
FIX: 01 §2, replace the Redis paragraph with: «İki Redis servisi. `redis-queue` (BullMQ, motor minimal durumu, bildirim ve hız limiti sayaçları): appendonly yes, appendfsync everysec, maxmemory 200mb, maxmemory-policy noeviction, konteyner limiti 256 MB. `redis-live` (ac:{hex}, GEO, ac:updates, ac:batch, izleyici bölgeleri): save "", appendonly no, maxmemory 200mb, maxmemory-policy volatile-ttl, konteyner limiti 256 MB. maxmemory her zaman konteyner limitinin en fazla %75'idir. Her XADD `MAXLEN ~ 50000` ile yapılır. XADD öncesi (hex, sampleTime) ile tekilleştir; aynı örnek iki çemberden gelirse bir kez yaz. Motor NOGROUP hatasında tüketici grubunu `XGROUP CREATE … MKSTREAM` ile yeniden kurar ve askıdaki mesajları XAUTOCLAIM ile alır. Her ingest turunda son görülmesi 120 sn'yi aşan GEO üyeleri ZREM ile silinir. used_memory/maxmemory %80'i geçince admin uyarısı gider.» ACTIVATION: «Host'ta `vm.overcommit_memory=1` ve 2 GB swap (Hostinger swap rehberi: https://support.hostinger.com/en/articles/8124185-how-to-set-up-swap-on-hostinger-vps). Bu ayarlar paylaşılan sunucudaki tüm projeleri etkiler; elle ve bilinçli uygulanır.»

### [critical] Deploys build Docker images on the shared 2-vCPU VPS; Dokploy's own docs advise against this (00-ortak-baglam 'Mimari ve teknoloji → Yayın'; 01-parca-1 §2 'Dockerfile'lar' and 'Üretim (docker-compose.yml, Dokploy)'; §10 deploy.yml and DEPLOY_DOKPLOY step 2)
PROBLEM: The prompts have Dokploy deploy the repo's docker-compose.yml. Dokploy runs `docker compose -p <app> up -d --build` (https://docs.dokploy.com/docs/core/docker-compose), so any `build:` key builds the whole monorepo on the VPS. A workspace `pnpm install` also pulls in apps/mobile (Expo/React Native) unless pruned. `next build` is known to be memory-hungry (https://nextjs.org/docs/app/guides/memory-usage), and compose builds services in parallel. The result is a multi-GB RAM spike and both vCPUs pegged for minutes (estimate) on a box shared with Dokploy, Traefik and other projects. Dokploy's docs say builds 'can lead to timeout on your server or even freezing your server' and recommend: 'Build & Publish the application in a CI/CD pipeline' (https://docs.dokploy.com/docs/core/applications/going-production). Dokploy Build Servers 'are currently only available for Applications… not supported for Docker Compose' (https://docs.dokploy.com/docs/core/remote-servers/build-server). While a build runs, ingest cycles slip (P0 freshness is missed, so notifications are late), and the kernel OOM killer may pick Postgres, the largest RSS.
FIX: 01 §2/§10, add: «Üretim imajları yalnızca GitHub Actions'ta derlenir ve GHCR'a gönderilir: `ghcr.io/<owner>/<app>-{web,api,worker}:sha-<kısa-sha>` ve `:main`. Her Dockerfile `turbo prune <app> --docker` ile başlar; mobil bağımlılıklar kuruluma ve imaja girmez. BuildKit cache `type=gha`, yalnızca linux/amd64. Üretim docker-compose.yml'inde hiçbir serviste `build:` yoktur. Uygulama servisleri `image: ghcr.io/…:${IMAGE_TAG:-main}` ve `pull_policy: always` kullanır. Yerel derleme ayrı `docker-compose.build.yml` ile yapılır. CI, `docker compose -f docker-compose.yml config` çıktısında `build:` bulursa kırmızıya döner. Dokploy'a GHCR 'Registry' kaydı yalnızca read:packages yetkili bir token ile eklenir. Dokploy 'Docker Cleanup' açık tutulur. `concurrency: cancel-in-progress` kullanılır ve yalnızca değişen uygulamanın imajı yeniden derlenir.» Cost note: GHCR 'Container image storage and bandwidth… is currently free', with at least 1 month's notice before any change (https://docs.github.com/en/billing/concepts/product-billing/github-packages). Private repos get 2,000 free Linux Actions minutes per month.

### [major] The memory budget does not account for Dokploy, other projects, swap or in-process settings on a shared KVM 2 (00-ortak-baglam 'Yayın' (KVM 2, 'her servise bellek limiti koy'); 01-parca-1 §2 'Bellek limitleri'; 04-parca-4 §4 'Kaynaklar ve yük testi')
PROBLEM: KVM 2 is 2 vCPU, 8 GB RAM, 100 GB NVMe and 8 TB traffic at $8.99/mo promo (https://www.hostinger.com/vps-hosting).
The limits sum to 1024+512+512+512+768 = 3,328 MB, plus the backup service and the migrate one-shot. Dokploy itself needs 'at least 2GB of RAM and 30GB of disk space' (https://docs.dokploy.com/docs/core/installation). The Dokploy app, its Postgres/Redis, the shared Traefik, the OS and dockerd come to about 1–1.5 GB (estimate). That leaves about 4.5–5 GB committed before other projects and before any build spike.
Limits are ceilings, not reservations, and swap is often absent on cloud images (verify on the box).
Inside the containers:
- The default Postgres config is not sized for 1 GB, and Docker's /dev/shm defaults to 64 MB, which parallel queries use.
- Node's heap is not tied to the cgroup limit unless set explicitly, so the kernel can kill a container before GC reacts.
- Worker 'all' at 768 MB mixes ingest JSON parsing, engine ring buffers, the daily OurAirports CSV import and the notifier.
FIX: 01 §2, add a 'Bellek bütçesi (KVM 2, 8 GB)' table to DEPLOY_DOKPLOY.md: «OS + Docker ~0,6 GB; Dokploy + Traefik ~1 GB; bu proje ≤3,3 GB; diğer projeler ≤2,5 GB; boşluk ≥0,6 GB; swap 2 GB.» Varsayılan limitler: «postgres 1024, redis-queue 256, redis-live 256, web 384, api 512, worker 640, backup 128 MB; postgres (512 MB) ve redis-queue (128 MB) için `mem_reservation`.» Postgres: «`shared_buffers=256MB effective_cache_size=640MB work_mem=4MB maintenance_work_mem=64MB max_connections=40 max_parallel_workers_per_gather=0 wal_compression=on max_wal_size=1GB`, `shm_size: 128mb`; api ve worker bağlantı havuzlarının toplamı ≤30.» Node: «Her Node servisi `NODE_OPTIONS=--max-old-space-size=<limitin %70'i>` ile çalışır.» 04 §4: «Yük testleri paylaşılan üretim VPS'inde değil, yerelde veya CI'da aynı limitlerle çalışır; ölçülen RSS değerleri rapora yazılır. KVM 4'e geçiş eşiği (4 vCPU / 16 GB / 200 GB): kalıcı bellek kullanımı %80'in üstünde ya da ingest döngü gecikmesi p95 > hedef × 1,5.»

### [major] The prompts never say who runs DB migrations at deploy time, when, or how races and backward-compatibility are handled (01-parca-1 §3 'Veritabanı' and §10 'CI/CD ve Dokploy'; applies to every later part that adds tables (02, 03, 04))
PROBLEM: There is no production migration step. drizzle-kit is a dev tool. If api and worker each migrate on boot, they race. If neither does, new code runs against the old schema. Compose recreates web/api/worker in no fixed order while old containers still serve, so breaking DDL breaks the running version. Drizzle has no first-class partitioning: feature request drizzle-orm#6235 (opened 2026-09-04, open) notes partitioned tables 'require custom SQL/migrations' (https://github.com/drizzle-team/drizzle-orm/issues/6235). If the partition job fails once, tomorrow's partition is missing and every INSERT into track_points fails from 00:00 UTC.
FIX: 01 §3, add: «Üretimde migration'ları yalnızca `migrate` adlı tek seferlik compose servisi çalıştırır: worker imajı, `command: ["node","packages/db/dist/migrate.js"]`, `restart: "no"`. drizzle-orm'un programatik migrator'ı kullanılır; drizzle-kit üretim imajında yoktur. api, worker ve web servisleri `depends_on: {migrate: {condition: service_completed_successfully}}` taşır. migrate `pg_advisory_lock` alır, `lock_timeout=5s` ve `statement_timeout=120s` ile çalışır; hata olursa sıfır olmayan kodla çıkar ve deploy durur. Migration'lar geriye uyumludur (expand → kodu yayınla → sonraki sürümde contract); sütun silme veya yeniden adlandırma tek deploy'da yapılmaz; bu kural DECISIONS'a yazılır. Partition DDL'i `packages/db/sql/` altında elle yazılmış, idempotent SQL'dir. migrate her çalışmada partition'ları 7 gün ileriye kadar açar. Bir DEFAULT partition bulunur; DEFAULT'a satır düşerse admin uyarısı verilir. CI iki test koşar: temiz DB'de tüm migration'lar, ve önceki sürüm şeması üzerine yeni migration'lar (yükseltme testi).»

### [major] The track_points retention design cannot work with daily partitions, and disk growth is unsized (01-parca-1 §3 'track_points' and 'Saklama varsayılanları'; 02-parca-2 §6 (takip listesi tam çözünürlük); 03-parca-3 admin 'disk kullanımı')
PROBLEM: The design mixes 7-day general rows and 30-day station/watchlist rows in the same daily partitions. Retention then can't be done with DROP PARTITION: it needs mass DELETEs (bloat, vacuum and IO on 2 vCPU), or everything is kept for 30 days. 'Yaklaşma yarıçapı' is undefined (the 60 NM P0 circle, or 50 km?). 'Tam çözünürlük' means every fetch, including parked ground targets and cruise overflights near IST.
Math (estimates): heap ≈95 B/row + (flight_id, ts) btree ≈35 B ≈ 130 B/row; about +80 B/row with a PostGIS geometry column and GiST index.
(A) Measured adsb.lol coverage: ~100 aircraft 24 h average, ~45 inside station circles, 4 s sampling. 100×2,880 = 0.29 M + 45×21,600 = 0.97 M ≈ 1.26 M rows/day ≈ 165 MB/day; steady state ≈ 31 M rows ≈ 4 GB.
(B) Design target: 1,500 peak / ~900 average, ~250 peak / ~150 average within 60 NM, 3 s sampling. 900×2,880 = 2.6 M + 150×28,800 = 4.3 M ≈ 6.9 M rows/day ≈ 0.9 GB/day (≈1.5 GB/day with geometry + GiST). Steady state 7×2.6 M + 30×4.3 M ≈ 148 M rows ≈ 19 GB (≈30 GB), plus WAL and backups, on a 100 GB disk shared with images and other projects. The 1,500 figure is only plausible for the whole 250 NM coverage union; IST's 3 runways allow 148 movements/h (AA source above).
FIX: 01 §3, replace the retention bullets with: «İki tablo. `track_points_sampled`: 30 sn örnekleme, günlük partition, 7 gün, DROP ile silinir. `track_points_fine`: günlük partition, 30 gün, DROP ile silinir. `track_points_fine`'a yalnızca şunlar yazılır: (a) bir istasyon için varış veya kalkış olarak sınıflanmış, ARP'a ≤30 km ve alan üstü <10.000 ft olan uçaklar; (b) takip listesindeki uçaklar. Örnekler arası en az 2 sn bırakılır. Yerde olup konumu 20 m'den az değişen örnekler yazılmaz; yalnızca durum değişimi yazılır. Tipler: lat/lon `double precision`, gs/track `real`, alt/baro_rate `integer`, source `smallint`. Geometry sütunu yoktur. Tek btree indeks `(flight_id, ts)`, ts üzerinde BRIN. İlk 24 saatten sonra `pg_total_relation_size` ile satır başına bayt ve günlük büyüme ölçülüp rapora yazılır. Admin panelinde disk kullanımı ve 'dolmaya kalan gün' gösterilir. Disk %75'te uyarı verilir; %90'da `track_points_fine` saklama süresi otomatik 14 güne iner ve admin'e e-posta gider.»

### [major] The backup plan fills the disk it runs on and does not protect against losing the VPS (04-parca-4 §4 'Yedekleme')
PROBLEM: A daily full pg_dump includes track_points (4–30 GB per the previous issue). That means long dumps and heavy CPU/IO on the shared 2 vCPU. Retention of 7 daily + 4 weekly is 11 copies. With the fallback 'S3 tanımlı değilse yerel volume', those 11 copies of several GB sit on the same 100 GB disk, so the backup job itself can fill the disk while giving no protection if the VPS is lost. Hostinger's included backups are weekly and VPS-level (https://www.hostinger.com/vps-hosting): a whole-server restore that affects all projects, with an RPO of up to 7 days. Dokploy Volume Backups warn of a 'risk of data corruption if the application is actively writing to the volume' unless the container is stopped (https://docs.dokploy.com/docs/core/volume-backups), so they are unsuitable for live Postgres. Dokploy's compose DB backup uses pg_dump -Fc and cannot exclude tables. Some secrets are not in the DB at all (VAPID private key, JWT keys, APNs .p8). Losing the VAPID key invalidates every web-push subscription.
FIX: 04 §4, replace with: «İki katmanlı yedek. (1) Günlük `pg_dump -Fc --exclude-table-data='track_points_*'` (hesaplar, takipler, olaylar, bildirimler, referans verisi) → Hostinger dışındaki bir S3 uyumlu depoya (Backblaze B2 veya Cloudflare R2); saklama 7 günlük + 4 haftalık. (2) İz verisi: günü kapanan partition bir kez `COPY … TO STDOUT | zstd` ile yüklenir ve 30 gün saklanır. S3 tanımlı değilse yerel yedek en fazla 2 kopya tutar, disk %70'in üstündeyse yazmaz ve admin panelinde kırmızı uyarı gösterilir. ### [major] WebSocket fan-out: per-client compression memory, no heartbeat or backpressure limits, and Traefik timeouts untested (01-parca-1 §7 'WS /v1/ws' (permessage-deflate açık, yavaş istemci); 02-parca-2 §8 'WS kanalları'; 04-parca-4 §4 (WS'de 1.000 istemci))
PROBLEM: The ws library (used by @fastify/websocket) disables permessage-deflate on the server by default and warns it 'adds a significant overhead in terms of performance and memory consumption'. It also warns of 'catastrophic memory fragmentation' under concurrency (https://github.com/websockets/ws). By zlib's formula, one deflate context is (1<<(windowBits+2)) + (1<<(memLevel+9)) = 256 KB at defaults, plus ~40 KB for inflate. With context takeover that is about 0.3 MB per connection, so 1,000 clients ≈ 300 MB of the 512 MB api limit (estimate). Computing, serializing and compressing a diff per client every second grows linearly with clients on 2 shared vCPUs. No heartbeat is specified, so half-open mobile sockets linger. Traefik's entrypoint readTimeout defaults to 60s (https://doc.traefik.io/traefik/v3.4/routing/entrypoints/) and is reported to cut long streams and uploads since v2.11.2. Its effect on upgraded WebSockets in Dokploy's Traefik is unverified and must be tested, and changing it affects every project behind that shared Traefik.
FIX: 01 §7, replace the WS bullets with: «Farklar istemci başına değil, ızgara hücresi başına üretilir. Hücreler zoom kademesine göre sabittir (ör. 4°, 1°, 0,25°). Her saniye her dolu hücre için fark bir kez serileştirilir (kompakt dizi) ve o hücreye abone tüm istemcilere aynı Buffer gönderilir. permessage-deflate varsayılan KAPALI'dır. Açılırsa şu ayarlarla açılır: `serverNoContextTakeover:true, clientNoContextTakeover:true, serverMaxWindowBits:10, zlibDeflateOptions:{memLevel:4,level:3}, threshold:1024, concurrencyLimit:4`; karar 1.000 istemcili yük testindeki RSS ölçümüne göre verilir. 25 sn'de bir ping atılır; 2 pong kaçıran bağlantı kapatılır. `bufferedAmount > 1 MB` ise istemciye ara farklar yerine yalnızca son tam durum gönderilir; `> 4 MB` ise bağlantı kapatılır. Sınırlar: IP başına 10 bağlantı, toplamda `WS_MAX_CONNECTIONS` (varsayılan 2.000).» DEPLOY_DOKPLOY doğrulama listesi: «Traefik üzerinden 5 dk boşta WS testi yapılır. 60. saniyede kopuyorsa Traefik entrypoint readTimeout ayarlanır; bu ayar sunucudaki tüm projeleri etkiler.»
